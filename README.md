📌 About the Project
Health Rich is an AI-powered healthcare platform that integrates nearby hospital discovery, senior citizen care, and pregnancy care into one accessible application. It aims to make healthcare more convenient, organized, and accessible for both rural and urban communities.

🎯 Problem Statement
People often rely on separate services to find hospitals, manage elderly care, and track pregnancy appointments, making healthcare coordination difficult.

💡 Our Solution
Health Rich provides three integrated modules:

1.🏥 Nearby Hospitals: Helps users discover nearby hospitals and healthcare facilities.
2.👴 Senior Care: Supports vitals tracking, medication reminders, routine checkups, and caregiver alerts.
3.🤰 Pregnancy Care: Offers personalized pregnancy schedules, antenatal appointment reminders, and educational guidance on warning signs.

✨ Key Features
1.Unified healthcare profiles
2.AI-powered multilingual health assistance
3.Location-based hospital discovery
4.Personalized health reminders
5.Vitals tracking and caregiver notifications
6.User-friendly interface for rural and urban communities

🛠️ Technologies
Planned technologies include:
1.Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript / React
2.Backend: Python / Flask
3.AI: AI-powered health information assistant
4.Maps: Google Maps API
5.Database: Firebase / Cloud database
The final technology stack will depend on implementation.

🌍 Target Users
1.Senior citizens and caregivers
2.Pregnant women and their families
3.Rural and urban communities
4.Individuals seeking nearby healthcare facilities

🔒 Safety and Privacy
Health Rich aims to protect personal health information through authentication, access controls, and secure data storage. AI provides general health guidance and does not replace professional medical advice or emergency services.

To make healthcare more connected, accessible, and convenient by bringing essential healthcare services together in one platform.
